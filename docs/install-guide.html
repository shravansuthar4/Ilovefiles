I love files

Drag any file, press C, drop it on a format, done. Install guide for Mac and Windows.

1. Pick the right installer from the folder

Mac with an Apple chipM1, M2, M3, M4 … (most Macs from 2021 on) I love files - Mac (Apple Silicon).dmg
Mac with an Intel chipOlder Macs I love files - Mac (Intel).dmg
Windows 10 or 11Any normal Windows PC I love files - Windows Setup.exe

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the Apple menu (top-left) → About This Mac. It says Chip: Apple M… or Processor: Intel.

Mac Install on a Mac

  1. Double-click the .dmg file. A window opens with the I love files icon and an Applications folder.
  2. Drag the I love files icon onto the Applications folder. That’s the whole installation.
  3. Open your Applications folder and double-click I love files.
    Your Mac may say it can’t verify the app. Click Done. That message is normal for apps shared outside the App Store.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and next to “I love files was blocked” click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open. (Only needed the first time.)
  5. When asked to allow Accessibility, click Open System Settings and switch on I love files. This lets the app notice the C key while you drag. If it also asks for Input Monitoring, switch that on too.
  6. A small ♡ heart appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen. You’re done: the app runs quietly and starts automatically when your Mac starts.
Tip: afterwards you can eject the “I love files” disk on the desktop or in the Finder sidebar, and delete the .dmg file.

Windows Install on Windows

  1. Double-click I love files - Windows Setup.exe.
  2. If a blue box says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info and then Run anyway. This appears for new apps that aren’t from the Microsoft Store.
  3. It installs by itself in a few seconds and starts right away. There’s nothing to click through.
  4. A pink heart appears in the system tray, bottom-right next to the clock (it may be hidden under the ^ arrow). The app starts automatically with Windows.

♡ How to use it

  1. Start dragging any file: a photo, song, video, Word document, PDF …
  2. While still holding the mouse button, tap C. A glass ring of formats appears around the file.
  3. Move onto the format you want: it turns blue.
  4. Let go. The converted file appears right next to the original. Your original is never changed.

Changed your mind? Let go in the middle of the ring, or press Esc. Big videos keep converting in the background and you get a notification when they’re ready.

What it converts

  • Photos & images: JPG, PNG, PDF, WEBP, GIF, AVIF, TIFF, BMP, ICO (also opens iPhone HEIC photos)
  • Music: MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, AIFF, WMA
  • Video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, WMV, plus video → GIF and video → MP3
  • Documents: Word → PDF / HTML / TXT, PDF → images or text, text & Markdown → PDF

For Excel, PowerPoint and older Word files, install the free LibreOffice (libreoffice.org). I love files picks it up automatically, and the ring shows a More… shortcut to it.

Something not working?

  • Mac, ring doesn’t appear: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility: make sure I love files is switched on (and under Input Monitoring, if listed). Click the ♡ heart in the menu bar to check its status.
  • Windows: drag files from File Explorer or the desktop; the ring appears where your mouse is.
  • Make sure you press C while the mouse button is still held down.

Uninstall

  • Mac: click the ♡ heart → Quit I love files, then drag the app from Applications to the Bin.
  • Windows: Settings → Apps → I love files → Uninstall.
